EEE Backup

Encrypted, versioned backups of the owner’s study folder to the owner’s Google Drive.

What it does

EEE Backup combines rclone for Google Drive access with Restic for encryption and versioned snapshots. It runs on the owner’s computer on a six-hour schedule when the computer and internet connection are available.

This application is for its owner’s personal use. It is not a public backup service and this website does not accept files or Google sign-ins.

Why Google access is requested

The desktop workflow needs permission to create, read and maintain its encrypted backup repository in Google Drive. The configured destination is EEE-Restic-Yedek. Google displays the actual permissions during authorization; the owner decides whether to grant them.

The configured Drive scope can technically access other Drive files. The backup commands are configured to use the dedicated repository. Google authorization is separate from the local Restic encryption key.
